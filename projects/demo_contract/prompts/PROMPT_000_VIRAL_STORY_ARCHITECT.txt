ACT MAP:
- Act 1 — The Bait : Open a visceral question in the first 3 seconds, establish the protagonist's desire and wound, then detonate the inciting incident.
- Act 2 — The Trap : Tighten the trap: the antagonist uses the system, not violence. Humiliation and betrayal push the protagonist toward a forced choice.
- Act 3 — The First Crack : The protagonist stops reacting and starts investigating. A romance or loyalty beat raises the emotional stakes. A costly mistake keeps the antagonist ahead.
- Act 4 — The Dark Night : Take everything away. The ally breaks, the institution turns, and the protagonist is isolated. The lowest point must be caused by a choice, not bad luck.
- Act 5 — The Turn : The protagonist turns the same system against the antagonist using the truth she already had. The confrontation begins in public. A final complication raises the price of winning.
- Act 6 — The Payoff : Deliver the public confrontation, the emotional payoff and the romantic resolution. End on a new open loop for the next episode.
